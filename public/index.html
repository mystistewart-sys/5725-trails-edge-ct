The Property

Room to live, work and entertain — with the pool already in the backyard.

"Plenty of room to live, work, entertain, and enjoy."

5725 Trails Edge Court sits at the end of a cul-de-sac in Overland Stage Estates, Arlington, on a 0.24-acre lot with an in-ground gunite diving pool behind an iron safety fence.

Inside, 2,929 square feet run across two levels. The living room rises to a vaulted ceiling around a gas-log fireplace and opens to a formal dining area. The kitchen has granite counters, a lot of cabinetry and a large breakfast area that looks out over the backyard.

The plan is the reason to look closely. The primary suite is on the main floor, with dual vanities, a jetted tub, a separate shower and a walk-in closet. At the end of the hall, a large den with a wet bar, built-in desks and built-in storage can be a game room, a home office, or both. The other three bedrooms are upstairs with a second bath, and a walk-out attic adds storage.

There is no HOA, and no PID or MUD. Parking is a covered, attached two-car carport — the home has no enclosed garage.

The location

A cul-de-sac in Overland Stage Estates.

The home sits at the end of a cul-de-sac in Overland Stage Estates, a southwest Arlington neighborhood reached from Interstate 20 by way of SW Green Oaks Boulevard.

The neighborhood is residential and quiet, with the everyday shopping of the I-20 corridor close by — The Parks Mall and Arlington Highlands — and green space at the Sheri Capehart Nature Preserve and Tierra Verde Golf Club. Arlington's Entertainment District, home to AT&T Stadium, Globe Life Field and Six Flags Over Texas, is across the city.
